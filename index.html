What are the most common reasons resumes get rejected?
CV
The four most frequent rejection triggers are:

1. Lack of measurable outcomes – Describing tasks instead of business impact.
2. Incompatible ATS formatting – Complex tables and multi-column designs.
3. Missing core competencies – Leaving out essential industry keywords.
4. Unclear scope – Vague ownership and generic responsibilities.
